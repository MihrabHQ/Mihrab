• iPad: Mihrab is now a full iPad app.
• Mac: Mihrab now also runs on Apple Silicon Macs.
• The layout adapts to the window: a two-column Home dashboard on wide screens, centred reading, and reflowing tools, Settings, Duas, Mosques, downloads, backup and month schedule, returning to a single column when narrow.
• Two facing mushaf pages in landscape.
• New tafsir selector in Settings that stays in sync with the Quran page, and your tafsir choice now sticks instead of resetting.
• Smoother day carousel on large screens: swiping works in landscape and each page shows exactly one day.
• The Qibla compass has been removed in this version.

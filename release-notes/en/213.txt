• More reliable prayer times for Sweden: they now come from a regularly refreshed set of Islamiska Förbundet times, work offline, and fall back gracefully if the network is unavailable, so your times always show.
• Karlshamn now uses the times for Sölvesborg, the nearest supported city (about 22 km away).
• A hidden data status card on Home, unlocked by tapping the version number five times, shows the prayer-time source, how far ahead times are stored and when they were last refreshed. It is translated into all app languages.

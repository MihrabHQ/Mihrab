• Sync now runs whenever you change your log, including from a notification or a widget with the app closed. It used to wait until you next opened the app.
• The refresh button on a widget syncs before it redraws, instead of showing you the same thing again.
• Removing a paired device now sticks: its file is deleted, it cannot add itself back, and your other devices drop it too.
• Sync setup says plainly that it needs a folder that Nextcloud, OneDrive, Dropbox or similar keeps in step.

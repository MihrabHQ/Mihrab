• Android: the next prayer on a Garmin watch, through Gadgetbridge. A watch face — the time as large as the dial allows, the date, today's steps, the weather, a battery bar under the clock, a ring from the last prayer to the next and the countdown — a data field for any activity screen, and a widget with complications for other faces. No Garmin account: the phone sends the times, and Settings → Devices → Garmin watch → Install or reinstall watch face puts the newest face on the watch through Gadgetbridge.
• Settings → Devices: pairing your other devices (Sync, which was under About) and the Garmin watch, together.
• Sync: a device that announces itself in the shared folder is no longer trusted on sight — it shows as waiting until you accept it. After scanning a code the pairing page keeps checking the folder by itself, so there is no need to press Sync again and again, and a device removed earlier can be paired again from either side.
• Storage: nothing sensitive falls back to an unencrypted copy, and the fallback that remains is kept out of device backups.
• The two security fixes above were reported by Ahmad Al-Khasawneh (github.com/ahmadkhasawneh92). Thank you.

• The Sky theme: the whole app painted from the sky over the countdown — night, dawn, morning, midday, the golden hour, dusk — changing through the day, in one look whether your phone is in light or dark mode. Settings → Appearance, in the first-run walkthrough, and offered once to everyone who was here before it.
• Full-screen prayer alerts take their prayer's hour under the Sky theme: Fajr's dawn, Dhuhr's blue, Asr's gold, Maghrib's magenta, Isha's indigo.
• The launch run is a setting, on by default: the app opens on the last Today screen and the countdown, the rail and the sky sweep up to now; off, the picture simply fades into the live one.
• Muṣḥaf: the fullscreen button is in the page bar and the page tone is in the header; the page's frame and marks take the page bar's own colour.
• Reading lock: its own switch on every platform, in Settings → Quran and the page bar. A locked page does not turn under your hand, but a tap on a word still opens its āyah; hold the lock to unlock (#75).
• Log: an optional "Mark late logs as late" — a log from a notification, the alarm or a widget in the second half of the window is recorded late (#74); with the Mālikī second times on, a log after the preferred time is the late one.
• Prayer-times widget (Android): with the times hidden, the next prayer fills the card.
• Sunnah: Isha carries one sunnah muʾakkadah, so the full rawātib make six (#73).
• Fixed: a square at the start of Fajr when the app reopened, a hiccup as the hero drew toward the next prayer, the Sky theme opening on three palettes, and a Live Activity card over the hero when the app opens.
